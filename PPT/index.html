02/WHAT IS NEIGHBOURAID
NeighbourAid03 / 10

Five ideas, finally combined.

You post what you see — text, GPS, a photo, or just your voice. The AI labels the urgency server-side in under a millisecond, then WebSockets page the skill-matched volunteers nearest you. Every alert is cross-checked against witnesses, live weather and the photo itself.
— 01 · CORE

Hyperlocal Dispatch

WebSockets page every volunteer within 5 km. That reaches 12 km if they have a vehicle, and 25 km when the skill is rare — CPR, driver.

FastAPI · WebSocket · 2dsphere
— 02

Instant AI Triage

A keyword classifier reads the report in any of our languages and labels it CRITICAL, HIGH, MED or LOW. No weights, no network call.

$0/request
— 03

Multi-source Verify

Four checks — witnesses, nearby reports, live weather, the photo — add up to one verified_score out of 100.

Capped per source · clamped 100
— 04

Built for India

Eleven languages including हिन्दी and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, the 112/108/101 dialer, and WhatsApp for anyone without the app.

11 speech locales
— 05

Free & Offline-tolerant

IndexedDB queue — a report survives a dead network and flushes on reconnect. Zero paid deps.

PWA · Service Worker

06/THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER
NeighbourAid07 / 10

AI triage + verification.

Before an alert reaches a single volunteer, it gets an urgency label and a trust score out of 100.

— AI urgency triage

A multilingual keyword classifier gets 38/40 right on the bundled eval set, in 0.03 ms median, with no weights and no network. Where it matches nothing — 8 reports out of 41 — a local Gemma 3 1B takes a second look. That lifts it to 39/40 and catches every implied-danger miss. It costs 2.8 s, so it runs in background enrichment and never on the request path.

09/WHAT'S NEXT
NeighbourAid10 / 10

Roadmap.

Six phases, from official disaster feeds to multi-region scale — Q2 2026 through 2027.
Phase 1
Q2 · 2026

NDRF / IMD feeds

Auto-generate alerts from official disaster feeds, so a district warning reaches a neighbourhood without anyone retyping it.

Phase 2
Q3 · 2026

Shared fan-out state

WebSocket connections and rate limits are per-process, so the API runs on one worker. Moving both behind shared storage is what lets it scale out.

Phase 3
Q4 · 2026

React Native app

Android-first and low-connectivity optimised. The PWA installs today; a native shell gets background location and a reliable alarm.

Phase 4
Q1 · 2027

Assamese · Urdu

Urdu needs right-to-left layout, which nothing in the UI does yet. Eleven languages ship today.

Phase 5
Q2 · 2027

Fine-tuned local model

The keyword classifier is 38/40 on the bundled eval. A small model trained on Indian crisis text is how the last two get caught.

Phase 6
H2 · 2027

Multi-region + E2E

Kubernetes across regions, and Playwright covering the whole dispatch path end to end.
