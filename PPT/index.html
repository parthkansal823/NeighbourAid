02/WHAT IS NEIGHBOURAID
NeighbourAid03 / 10

Five ideas, finally combined.

Reporters post (text · GPS · photos · voice). AI triages server-side in under a millisecond. Skill-matched volunteers within 5 km are paged over WebSockets — every alert cross-checked against witnesses, weather, and photo evidence.
— 01 · CORE

Hyperlocal Dispatch

WebSocket fan-out to volunteers within a 5 km radius. Auto-extends to 15 km on rare skill match (CPR, driver, etc.).

FastAPI · WebSocket · 2dsphere
— 02

Instant AI Triage

Multilingual keyword classifier → CRITICAL / HIGH / MED / LOW. No weights, no network.

$0/request
— 03

Multi-source Verify

Composite verified_score (0–100): witnesses + corroboration + weather + photos.

Capped per source · clamped 100
— 04

Built for India

11 languages incl. हिन्दी / ਪੰਜਾਬੀ + 112/108/101 dialer + WhatsApp inbound.

en-IN · hi-IN · pa-IN

06/THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER
NeighbourAid07 / 10

AI triage + verification.

Every alert gets an urgency label and a 0–100 trust score before broadcast.

— AI urgency triage

Multilingual keyword classifier — 38/40 on the bundled eval set at 0.03 ms median, no weights, no network. A local Gemma 3 1B is consulted only where the classifier matched nothing (8 of 41 reports), taking it to 39/40 and fixing every implied-danger miss. It costs 2.8 s, so it runs in background enrichment, never on the request path.

09/WHAT'S NEXT
NeighbourAid10 / 10

Roadmap.

From feeds to fine-tuning to multi-region scale — the next twelve months.
Q2 · 2026

NDRF / IMD feed

Auto-generate alerts from official disaster feeds.

Q3 · 2026

Shared fan-out state

WebSocket and rate limits are per-process, so the API runs on one worker. Moving both behind shared storage is what lets it scale out.

Q3 · 2026

Assamese · Urdu

Urdu needs RTL layout, which nothing else in the UI does yet. Eleven languages ship today.

Q4 · 2026

React Native app

Android-first, low-connectivity optimised.
